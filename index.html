Stream Highlighter

A Windows desktop app that turns highlight moments from live streams into short vertical (9:16) videos and lets you review and publish them to your own TikTok account.

  • Finds highlight moments from live chat activity.
  • Builds vertical clips with the streamer's webcam and gameplay.
  • You review every clip, edit the caption, choose privacy and interaction settings, and only then publish it with TikTok's Content Posting API.

Everything runs locally on your computer. Privacy Policy · Terms of Service
