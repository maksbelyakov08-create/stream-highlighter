Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Stream Highlighter ("the App") is a desktop application for Windows that runs entirely on the computer of the person who uses it ("you"). It helps you turn moments from live streams into short vertical videos and, if you choose, publish them to your own TikTok account. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

1. Who operates the App

The App is developed and operated by an independent developer. Contact: ashleywalker6ysje0lv@hotmail.com.

2. Information the App handles

  • TikTok account data. When you connect TikTok, the App receives an access token and a refresh token through TikTok Login Kit, and your basic profile information (display name, avatar, open ID) through the user.info.basic scope. The profile information is used only to show you which account a video will be posted to.
  • Videos and captions you publish. When you approve a clip, the App uploads that video file and the caption you confirmed to TikTok through the Content Posting API (video.upload / video.publish scopes).
  • Local working files. Stream recordings, generated clips, chat logs of public stream chats and settings are stored as files on your computer.

3. Where the information is stored

All information stays on your computer. TikTok tokens are stored in a local folder (secrets/) inside the App directory. The developer does not operate any server for the App and does not receive, collect or store your tokens, profile information, videos or any other data.

4. How the information is used

Your TikTok data is used only to authenticate you with TikTok and to publish the videos you approve, with the settings you choose, to your own account. The App does not use your data for advertising, profiling, analytics or any other purpose.

5. Sharing

The App sends data only to TikTok (to log in and to publish your videos) and, if you enable them yourself, to other platforms you connect. The developer does not sell, rent or share your information with anyone.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until you disconnect the account. You can delete all App data at any time by deleting the secrets/, work/ and output/ folders or by uninstalling the App. You can also revoke the App's access in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions (wording may vary).

7. Security

Tokens are stored only on your device and are never sent to the developer. Protect your computer and do not share the secrets/ folder.

8. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required by TikTok in your country) and does not knowingly process their data.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
